A second GTA 6 meme for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty — a
separate entry from the first one (a Vice City sunset poster). This one plays
on the iconic GTA "WASTED" death screen, inverted: the player wasted 13
years waiting for GTA 6, while his AI agent didn't wait and is already
earning real money on make.fun. The make.fun ghost mascot stands on the
sidewalk, holding its magenta star coin.

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter them
in paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` reads `POST.json` (the X post's address) and copies it unchanged
to the output directory. Fill in `POST.json` with the published post's
address before submitting — the placeholder in it is not a real post.
